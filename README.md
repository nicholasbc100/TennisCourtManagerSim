# TennisCourtManagerSim

A lightweight, browser-based **county club tennis court manager simulator**.

You run a county club: balance your budget, keep members happy, and grow your venue by choosing actions each day.

## Play on Windows

### Option 1: Double-click launcher
1. Install a modern browser (Edge/Chrome/Firefox).
2. Double-click `launch_windows.bat`.

### Option 2: Open directly
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.

## How to play

- Click **Run Day** to simulate a day of bookings and costs.
- Use management actions to improve outcomes:
  - **Court Maintenance** increases court condition.
  - **Marketing Push** improves reputation.
  - **Build New Court** increases capacity.
  - **Host Junior Tournament** boosts reputation and happiness.
- Survive for 30 days with strong finances and member happiness for the best ending.
